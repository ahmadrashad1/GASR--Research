Module 5 & 6 Architecture

How the conditional diffusion model is built, how it is trained, and how Module 6 queries it for future tissue geometry.

Fig 1

The ConditionalDenoiser

Two encoders run in parallel — one over the four past frames, one over the noisy displacement being denoised. Both are conditioned, then fused by cross-attention that runs in both directions over the 512-point axis.

Fig 2

What it is trained on

The network above is one step inside a diffusion objective. Two rescalings stand between Module 4's raw displacement and the target the model actually sees — and both are inverted before any metric is computed, so evaluation always happens in physical units.

Fig 3

Module 6 — inference architecture

Module 6 trains nothing. It rebuilds the exact trajectory Module 5 saw, proves that it did so, and then drives one shared sampler in three different ways.

Direct prediction vs rollout

The two studies reach the same horizon by different routes, and the difference is one edge.
